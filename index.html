WHY COGNITA

Cognita is designed as an adaptive competency system rather than a course catalogue. It combines diagnostics, guided learning, authentic performance tasks, explanatory feedback, and mastery-based progression.

Francine Marie Bautista brings experience across education, training, communications, business, and technology. The pilot is designed to create measured Cebu evidence rather than rely on invented claims of prior local outcomes.

RESPONSIBLE BY DESIGN

BUILD CAPABILITY
WITHOUT CREATING
NEW GOVERNANCE RISK.

PRIVACY

Collect only what the pilot needs.

Participant data handling will be purpose-limited, transparent, and designed around the Data Privacy Act and NPC AI guidance.

DATA

Cebu keeps useful program intelligence.

The Province receives agreed pilot findings, reports, and anonymized or aggregated outputs needed for public decision-making.

IP

No hidden ownership trap.

Cognita retains its pre-existing platform, methodology, and core IP. Cebu-specific materials, access rights, and licensing are defined clearly in the pilot agreement.

ACCOUNTABILITY

AI does not become the decision-maker.

Human verification, escalation, and accountability remain built into learning and program governance.

 Development Academy of the Philippines · 25 August 2026AI course for local government units
